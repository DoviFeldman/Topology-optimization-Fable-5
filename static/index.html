🧠 How this works, in one minute
The optimizer keeps only the material that carries force from the orange spot to the blue anchors, then smooths the survivor into a printable shell.

Blobs? Force too spread out (raise concentration via a smaller force area) or too much material kept.
Just a stick? One force direction only needs one strut — turn up Internal webbing and/or Keep outer shape.
Whole shape vanished? Raise Keep outer shape: it puts a floor under the surface skin so your silhouette survives.
Webs too chunky? More Resolution, less Keep material.
Contact surfaces are sacred: the blue anchor faces and the orange force spot always keep a flat, solid pad (at least min strut thick) — the part still sits flat and bolts on exactly like the original; only the material between the pads gets carved.
